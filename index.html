个人简介(Biography)

Biography

刘双，蚂蚁集团机密计算团队负责人。带领团队自主研发开放通用 TEE 架构 HyperEnclave，使基于虚拟化与 TPM 的跨平台可信执行环境兼容既有 SGX 生态，技术方案已在金融、隐私计算等场景规模化落地。相关研究成果发表于 ASPLOS、USENIX ATC、NDSS 等系统与安全领域顶级会议（均为 CCF-A 类）。

团队产出的「Asterinas 机密计算」入选工信部典型应用案例，「密态时空计算」入选国家数据局典型应用案例。

加入蚂蚁集团之前，在芯片公司深耕可信执行环境、虚拟化、操作系统等根技术领域 10 余年。

Shuang Liu is the Director of Confidential Computing at Ant Group. He leads the team that developed HyperEnclave, an open, universal TEE built on virtualization and TPM that runs existing SGX workloads across heterogeneous platforms; the technology has been deployed at scale in finance and privacy-computing scenarios. Research from the team appears at top systems & security venues including ASPLOS, USENIX ATC, and NDSS (all CCF-A).

The team's "Asterinas Confidential Computing" was selected as a typical application case by China's MIIT, and "Confidential Spatio-Temporal Computing" was selected as a typical application case by the National Data Administration.

Before joining Ant Group, he spent more than 10 years at a chip company working on the root technologies of trusted execution environments, virtualization, and operating systems.
