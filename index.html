Biography

Shuang Liu is the Director of Confidential Computing at Ant Group. He leads the team that developed HyperEnclave, an open, cross-platform trusted execution environment built on virtualization and TPM that runs existing SGX workloads on heterogeneous hardware; the technology has been deployed at scale in finance and privacy-computing scenarios. Research from the team appears at top systems and security venues, including ASPLOS, USENIX ATC, and NDSS (all CCF-A).

The team's "Asterinas Confidential Computing" was selected as a typical application case by China's MIIT, and "Confidential Spatio-Temporal Computing" was selected as a typical application case by the National Data Administration.

Before joining Ant Group, he spent more than 10 years at a chip company working on the root technologies of trusted execution environments, virtualization, and operating systems.
